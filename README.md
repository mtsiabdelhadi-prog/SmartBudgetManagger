# SmartBudget Manager

An Excel workbook that helps you **record monthly income and expenses, sort them by category, see your balance automatically, and get a warning when you exceed your budget.**

*Author: NameOfStudent – Group · Version 1.0 · File: `SmartBudget_Manager.xlsx`*

## What you need
- Microsoft Excel 2016, 2019, 2021 or Microsoft 365 (or a compatible spreadsheet application)
- The file `SmartBudget_Manager.xlsx` – no installation, no macros, works offline
- Basic Excel skills: open a file, click a cell, type, use a drop-down list

## What is inside
| Worksheet | Use it to |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Read total income, total expenses, balance, budget status and the pie chart |
| **Transactions** | Enter every income and expense (rows 4–50) |
| **Categories** | See the five categories (Food, Transport, Rent, Health, Education) and set their monthly budget |

## Quick start
1. Open `SmartBudget_Manager.xlsx`. If Excel shows *Protected View*, click **Enable Editing**.
2. Go to the **Categories** sheet and type your monthly budget for each category in **Monthly Budget**.
3. Go to the **Transactions** sheet. In the first empty row enter the **Date**, a **Description**, the **Type** (*Income* or *Expense*) and, for an expense, the **Category**, all from the drop-down lists.
4. Type the amount in the **Income** column (for income) **or** the **Expense** column (for an expense).
5. Open the **Dashboard**: totals, balance and chart update by themselves. **OVER BUDGET** (red) means your expenses are higher than your total budget.

## Good to know
- Do **not** type in the **Month** column or over formulas – they are automatic.
- Only rows 4 to 50 are counted (47 transactions). Use one file per month.
- Choose categories from the list; a typed or blank category will not appear in the category table or chart.
- The Dashboard shows the first five categories only.
- Save often (**Ctrl+S**) and keep a backup copy.
